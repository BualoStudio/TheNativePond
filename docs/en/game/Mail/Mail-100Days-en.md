Dear Player:

Before you know it, you have spent a hundred days at The Native Pond.

You have been through so much at The Native Pond. You have been caught in the rain, and you have waited for the bright, clear sky that follows. You have been thrilled by a fish that finally took the hook after a long wait, and delighted by a taste of something delicious blooming on your tongue. Sometimes you linger by the pond's edge, quietly watching that stillness where a gentle breeze blows softly and the water stays unruffled; sometimes you wander about, discovering the beauty of every flower and blade of grass; and sometimes you simply sit still and meditate, savouring a day that was tiring yet full.

I am honoured that The Native Pond could bring you a hundred days of beauty, and honoured that it could bring you a hundred hours of comfort.

Perhaps life in another world is not as beautiful as life at The Native Pond, but when the sun of a new day rises, when flowers bloom by the roadside, when a gentle breeze threads through the woods, when spring rain falls in a soft patter upon the earth, when the warmth of a meal lingers at the corner of your mouth, when a little cat wanders into your life by the roadside, when you step across soft, springy grass, when you look up and see the stars in the night sky — I hope you will remember the beauty of The Native Pond, and reignite your drive for life.

In the future, you may keep staying at The Native Pond, or you may only come back to visit from time to time. Even if one day you leave The Native Pond forever, we will be glad that this pond once held your story. But no matter what, whenever you need it, The Native Pond will always be here waiting for you.

The Native Pond Production Team
