Dear Player:

Welcome to The Native Pond. Arriving at this pond for the first time, you must be full of curiosity. You can wander around the pond, go fishing every now and then, and tend to the fields; or you can stay in the camp, cook, admire the flowers, or simply lie on the sofa. Here, you are free — there is no need to hurry for anything. The vast pond will carry all the feelings in your heart.

We hope this little corner of the world can become a place for you to pause along your journey.

The Native Pond Production Team
