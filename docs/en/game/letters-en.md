# Letters

> [!NOTE]
> This file is used to archive all letters that appear in the game.

1. Stars dot the sky, and a gentle breeze passes over the stillness. On this tranquil night, a thousand beauties will naturally surface in your heart.
2. The moment you begin to chase the light, you become the light.
3. Hello. I don't know who you are, and I have no precious gift to give you. But I can give you a little red flower, and a smile. Remember to be happy every day~(◕ᴗ◕✿)!
4. When a meteor falls from the sky, it does not mean an ending — it means it has created a beauty that belongs only to itself.
5. Waiting for the spring breeze, for the summer rain, for the winter jasmine to bloom across the land. Waiting for the birdsong, for the chirping of insects, for the twinkling of stars and the stillness of the night sky. Waiting for the school bell to ring and the joyous clamour of voices, for the train to arrive and the reunion of laughter and tears. Waiting for the sound of fireworks to pierce the silence of a snowy night, as people bless one another and hold each other close. Waiting for the moment when every beauty of the world arrives — and the smiles that we wear together on our faces.
6. Perhaps there are five thousand flowers in the world exactly like yours, but only you are the one unique rose. — The Little Prince
7. What is happiness? It playfully hides in piles of fallen leaves, ripples across the edge of the pond, grows between the buds of the silk tree, is passed along by crickets among the fragrant grass, melts on a fifty-cent popsicle, rises with the smoke of a barbecue stall, and quietly tucks itself into the bottom of your heart — appearing in every moment you feel with your whole heart.
8. When I sit alone by the pond, I always pour my whispers into it. Perhaps the water, with its vast embrace, can hold all the thoughts in the world.
9. Even the most beautiful flower was once just a new sprout. All beauty requires waiting; all blossoms require time.
10. When a leaf falls, it has not been abandoned by spring — it is playing the music of autumn.
11. In the long river of time, every individual is a surging wave, rushing ceaselessly onward. No one can grasp a wave, but memory can engrave the sound of the waves.
12. You need not grow wings to embrace the sky, nor fins to embrace the sea.
13. People always long for the sun in the sky, yet often overlook the moonlight in the dew.
14. Perhaps beauty is not the absence of imperfection, but finding the beauty of fragments within it.
15. Across the pond, is there someone sharing the same evening breeze with me?
16. Hundreds of millions of years from now, at the other end of the universe, will there be a child lying on the grass, gazing at a sky full of stars from hundreds of millions of years ago — and happening to be looking at me, from hundreds of millions of years ago, at this very moment?
17. There is only one heroism in the world: to see the world as it is, and to love it. — Romain Rolland
18. Life goes on, and the sun will still rise as always. Tomorrow is another new day.
19. Traveller, do not fret over standing still. Only one who is fully prepared can journey farther.
20. Everyone has many things that go wrong in their life — one finishes, and another comes. So the lilac knots come every year. Knots can never be fully untied; neither can the problems of life. Otherwise, would life not be too plain and tasteless? — Zong Pu, 'The Lilac Knot'
21. Time engraves every precious moment. Like the rings of a tree — those are the memorials of their lives.
22. Keep smiling. Look — when you smile, this pond is smiling back at you.
23. In the vast universe there are countless stars, and when you look up at the starry sky, there is always one star that belongs only to you.
24. Life itself is created amid countless miracles. If one day you find yourself at a "dead end", I believe you will surely write a "miracle of life".
25. If life seems meaningless, then why not let every minute and every second of it be given meaning by you.
26. Do not fret over a small patch of cloud. Open your view — a whole vast sky is waiting for you.
27. To some, hope may be just a single lonely lamp. Yet the faint glimmer it gives off is enough to guide the way through the dark. Choosing to hold fast to hope is the first step toward the light.
28. It is precisely because of the drops and falls that a river gains the magnificence of its surging course.
29. The unknown is often what worries people most. Yet what would be the point of a life that is certain and unchanging? What truly makes life life is its enchanting unpredictability.
30. Compared with the vast unit of time that is "a lifetime", the trivialities around us are only temporary. Do not be shackled by small things — the road ahead is still long.
31. You need not force yourself to bloom as a rose — everyone has their own unique radiance.
32. Life comes into this world through a coincidence of one in hundreds of millions, or one in trillions. That is to say, you yourself are the most precious gift in this world.
33. The protagonist is not only the one shining on the stage. When you give your all to live your own life, you, too, are a dazzling protagonist.
34. At the very least… the sunset is beautiful, isn't it?
35. I understand that facing life's trials and still holding on until today is no easy feat. You are truly brave.
36. Everyone has their moments of triumph and of setback, like the moon in the sky — sometimes full, sometimes waning. Yet isn't that very cycle of fullness and waning the moon's unique beauty? Cherish the fullness, and do not suffer over the waning.
37. Life is inherently a journey without a fixed destination. We need not reach an end point — experiencing the journey itself matters more than chasing its terminus.
38. When the dandelion flies into the distance, it does not know where it is going. But wherever it lands, it will strive to take root and sprout.
39. Sometimes it is better to be a little more lenient with yourself. There is no need to act strong all the time — leave yourself some room, and put yourself back together.
40. Face the self you once were with equanimity, and say goodbye to that self. This is part of growing up.
41. The laughter and tears of the day will all gather in your dreams into a new story. Leave yesterday to your dreams — today is another brand-new day.
42. Emotions are the tide — they come, and they go. But you are the shore, always here.
43. No matter what happens, those 2 billion warm cells deep in your heart are still beating for you.
