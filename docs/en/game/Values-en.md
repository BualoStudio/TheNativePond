## ✨ What is the meaning of The Native Pond?

In real life, people are always swept along by all kinds of tasks, pressures and responsibilities, and these often leave them physically and mentally exhausted — even lost, unsure of their direction and self-worth. We believe it is time to carve out a pond where everyone can slow down in this fast-paced era. The Native Pond is a haven for the soul — it is not large, but it is large enough to hold all your emotions and thoughts. To that end, we define The Native Pond as a utopia: within it, nothing bad can exist or happen. It is a spiritual rest stop carrying peace, love and hope. **The meaning of The Native Pond is to give everyone a place of momentary rest and healing in the face of any difficulty, setback or pain in reality, so that they may continue facing life with a fuller spirit.** The game will constantly remind players that "this is just a game", with the aim of preventing players from becoming so attached to The Native Pond that they escape and resist the real world.

---

## 👟 How is The Native Pond operated?

We have noticed that most games today are filled with dreary content such as daily check-ins, quest systems and dungeon grinding. We believe that mechanisms that force players to stay online for long hours every day and perform highly repetitive actions have long since betrayed the original purpose of games. A game should be a way for players to have fun and relax, not a tool for game companies to make money by fleecing players. To that end, The Native Pond Production Team promises: **this project's source code is open source, and it will be released free on all platforms, with no ads of any kind inside the game, and no paid DLC or microtransactions of any form.** We believe what players need is genuinely good games, not shoddy products that chase short-term profits while disregarding the feelings of players.
